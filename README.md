# SENSOTECH — Fresh Farmer Intelligence Platform

A new production-oriented foundation for the SENSOTECH farmer app.

## Product principle
Deep intelligence inside. Extremely simple outside.

## Initial scale target
1,000 farmers, with an architecture designed to scale horizontally later.

## Primary farmer navigation
- Home / Today
- My Farm
- Scan
- Ask SENSOTECH
- Market
- News
- More

## Home behavior
The Home screen answers: **What matters for my farm right now?**

After the important actions and shortcuts, the farmer can scroll down to the **Live Soil & Sensor Data** section when a SENSOTECH sensor is connected. It includes N, P, K, pH, EC, moisture, soil temperature, connection/health and last-update information.

Without a connected sensor, the app must not invent sensor readings. It instead presents available Soil Intelligence from permitted satellite, weather, farm-history and agricultural data.

## Product scope
The build will cover the complete SENSOTECH capability map: farm context, sensors, satellite, camera AI, SENSOTECH AI/voice, weather-to-action, water intelligence, pest/disease, crop intelligence, farm planning, inputs, money/profit, market/selling, harvest/post-harvest, machinery/labour, government, farmer news, farm records, local intelligence, emergency/risk, offline/low-data experience, advanced decision systems and the revenue ecosystem.

See `docs/MASTER_PRODUCT_SPEC.md` for the authoritative product contract.

## Build rule
This repository is a fresh project. Do not copy the old SENSOTECH application codebase into it. External AI coding agents may work sequentially against this repository, but they must preserve the architecture and contracts documented here.
